# Security review — M4 Respin vote

Date: 14 November 2026. Scope: the `vote` message, the vote and result
phases, respins, closing after the final result, and the vote screens.

## What changed

- After every spin a 30-second vote opens for everyone connected at that
  moment. More than half yes: the winner is removed from this room (with its
  boosts) and the reel spins again by itself. At most 3 respins; no vote when
  fewer than 2 restaurants would remain or nobody is connected.
- The vote ends early as soon as the outcome is certain. Votes can be
  changed until then. Not voting, leaving and being removed count as no.
- After the final result the room closes 5 minutes later (reason `finished`).
- Phases are now `lobby` → `spinning` → `voting` → (`spinning` …) → `result`.
- App: vote panel under the result card (Ja/Nee with `aria-pressed`,
  countdown from the server's remaining time, tally), latecomer notice,
  toasts for the outcome, closing time in the header.

## Findings

| #   | Finding                                                                              | Severity | Status                                                                                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| F1  | Removing a participant mid-vote could shrink the electorate and make a respin easier | Low      | Fixed: the electorate is fixed at the start; a removed voter's vote is dropped and counts as no       |
| F2  | The host can block a respin by removing yes-voters during the vote                   | Info     | Accepted: the host already controls the room, and every removal is announced to everyone              |
| F3  | Votes are open: every phone receives who voted yes and who voted no                  | Info     | Accepted for a team lunch; can be made secret later by sending only counts plus your own vote         |
| F4  | Someone could join under several names before the spin to get more votes             | Info     | Accepted: needs the team key, names are visible in the room, host can remove them, 20 people per room |
| F5  | On a 360 px phone the vote panel was below the fold after the reel stopped           | Info     | Fixed: the result card scrolls into view when it appears                                              |

## Residual risk

Low. A client can only say yes or no for itself, once, during a vote it was
eligible for; counting, the deadline and what happens next are decided by
the room on the server.
